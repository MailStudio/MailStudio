Welcome to MailStudio

Sign in to Outlook just like you would in a browser — Mail, Calendar, Teams and To Do in one window. One sign-in carries across every Microsoft surface. API notifications are optional: they power richer notifications but need a free Azure/Asana app registration.

Connect your accounts

Notifications stay silent until you connect at least one account. Sign in once — tokens are stored in your OS keychain and refresh automatically. Connecting also fills your sidebar feeds with recent unread mail and upcoming calendar events, no setup needed.

This needs your own (free) Azure / Asana app registration — about 5 minutes. Read the setup guide.
